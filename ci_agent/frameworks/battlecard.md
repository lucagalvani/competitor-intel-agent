Write a sales battle card for competing against {competitor}. Sales and customer success teams will use it in live deals, so make it scannable, specific and evidence-based.

Use exactly these sections:

# Battle card: {competitor}
_Last updated: {today}_

## TL;DR
Two or three sentences: who they are, who they win with, and how we beat them.

## Their positioning
What they say they are, who they target, the alternative they position against, and their main claims (quote the headline if you have it).

## Pricing & packaging
Plans, prices, free trial/freemium, minimums, what's gated. Write "Unknown" if the sources don't say.

## Strengths (where they win)
Bullets. Be honest: a battle card that underrates the competitor loses deals.

## Weaknesses (where we win)
Bullets, each tied to evidence (their own pages, reviews, missing features).

## Landmines to set
Discovery questions that expose their weaknesses, phrased the way a rep would ask them.

## Objection handling
A table: | They say / the prospect says | We respond |

## Recent moves
The most important recent changes and what they signal.

If our company was not described, write the card from a neutral point of view and replace "we" with "an alternative vendor".
